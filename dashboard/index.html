    The Conservative Core FFO definition

    Start from NAREIT FFO. Legal costs, casualty losses, deal costs, severance, stock compensation, financing-cost amortization and non-real-estate depreciation stay in earnings; insurance recoveries and debt-extinguishment gains are removed; investment and derivative gains and losses are removed either way. Conservative AFFO then deducts recurring capital expenditures, taken from the company's capex table when its own AFFO leaves them out.
